Artist Statement

I am a Greek artist based in Athens and in Paros known for my acrylic paintings of creatures, also my sculptures, my digital artworks, and mixed media art. My art flows through themes like the alien creatures, their environment, and their everyday life. Other subjects like the pollution of earth, control, portraits, readymade sculptures, landscapes, body, diversity, and documentary photography. The mediums I use are acrylic paint for the paintings with some of them to have also ink or clay my sculptures have a variety of clay, metal, liquid glass, concrete and fabrics. The style I use in my artworks are surrealism, abstract and realism. I am influenced by cartoons and books also my experience from my childhood as a queer human in an island. Some of my favorite artists are Dr Seuss, Chris Sanders, Erika Sanada, Peca, Jesus Aguado, Hieronymus Bosch, Francis Bacon, H.R. Giger, Patricia Piccinini and Thomas Grünfeld. My paintings explore every dimension of life, sex, and environments through the cosmos. In a world dominated by control, I want my art to be a free landscape of diversity who humans can identify with.
